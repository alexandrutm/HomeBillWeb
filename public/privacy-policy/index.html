Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the HomeBill website or use the HomeBill app.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Information in the HomeBill app

HomeBill does not require an account. Bills, transactions, amounts, dates, preferences, and other information you enter in the app are stored locally on your device. HomeBill does not operate a cloud account or receive this app data. HomeBill does not connect to your bank or make payments.

Your device and operating system control access to locally stored app data. If you uninstall the app, change devices, or reset your device, local data may be lost. Backups, if enabled through your device or operating system, are governed by that provider's settings and privacy terms.

Optional device sync

If you choose to pair devices and sync, HomeBill transfers selected transaction records directly between your paired devices over your local network. Sync is started by you, requires the other device to be running HomeBill, and does not use a HomeBill server, cloud relay, or account. Sync data is encrypted in transit. Your devices' local network addresses and the records you choose to sync are used to establish and perform that connection. Only pair devices you own or trust.

Pairing credentials are stored in the platform's secure storage where available. You can remove a paired device in the app. Local network access is used only when you start pairing or sync.

Notifications and camera access

If you enable reminders and grant the requested operating-system permission, HomeBill schedules notifications on your device. Reminder information is used locally to deliver those notifications; HomeBill does not use a remote push-notification service.

On platforms where QR scanning is available, you may grant camera access to scan a device-pairing invitation. The camera is used for that scan; HomeBill does not use it for advertising or analytics. You can also choose not to use camera-based pairing.

Website and service providers

When you visit this website, your browser sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, browser and device information, and the pages requested. The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages; Cloudflare may process technical information to deliver, secure, and operate the site under its own privacy terms.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those fonts, and Google may receive technical connection information. See the Google Privacy Policy.

HomeBill does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics tools on this website. The site does not ask you to submit financial information.

External links and support messages

The website and app may link to third-party services, including Buy Me a Coffee. If you follow one of these links, that service handles information according to its own privacy policy. HomeBill does not receive your payment details from Buy Me a Coffee.

If you email support@homebill.eu, HomeBill receives your email address and the contents of your message so it can respond. Please do not include sensitive financial information in a support email. Messages are kept only as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or as required by law.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what information to enter in HomeBill, whether to use device sync, and whether to grant optional camera or notification permissions. You can manage permissions through your device settings and remove local app data using the app or device controls available to you.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact support@homebill.eu. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

HomeBill is a general household finance tracking tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not send children's personal information to HomeBill through support email.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or app changes. The date at the top indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
